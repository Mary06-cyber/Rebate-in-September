## 🧠 How to choose a trader

| Metric | What to check |
|---|---|
| Long-term ROI | Prefer 3–6 months or longer; ignore one-week spikes |
| Win rate | Combine with profit/loss ratio; high win rate with small rewards may still lose |
| Maximum drawdown | Deeper drawdown means larger margin and psychological risk; filter drawdown before chasing return |
| Profit-share ratio | Futures/elite copy commonly 10%–20% from realized profit; some elite tiers up to 20%, private/CFD portfolios may allow other ranges—confirm trader page [5](@ref) |
| Copiers count | Too many copiers may affect slippage and execution; moderate is usually better |
| Leverage habit | Check the trader’s usual futures leverage and set your own lower cap |
| Style | Trend, swing, scalping, grid or spot DCA should match your risk tolerance |
| Transparency | Public history, open positions policy, settled profit-share records, high-water-mark or stated settlement rule |

Rule: filter unacceptable drawdown first, then long-term consistency, then return. The number-one trader on the board is not always the best fit.
