## 🏦6. What Are Binance bStocks?

bStocks are tokenized securities for eligible users in permitted jurisdictions, issued by BTech Holdings Limited (Binance group affiliate), described as backed 1:1 by underlying via custody, legally "certificates representing specific financial instruments", NOT shares themselves.

Traits:
- fractional from small amounts (~5 USDT min)
- spot via stablecoin pairs
- 24/7 on Binance spot infra for supported products
- near-instant settlement
- BNB Smart Chain token structure (BEP-20, MUB contract above)
- corporate actions via bStocks framework (dividend reinvest, split adjusts multiplier)
- withdrawable to compatible BNB Chain wallets for on-chain use and some DeFi

Still securities products; not universal; eligibility = jurisdiction + regulation (US persons not eligible, also Canada, Cuba, Iran, Netherlands, North Korea, Syria, US territories restricted).
