## 6️⃣ What Is "Net Deposit"? Does Withdrawal Affect Rewards?

Many new users see "Deposit" and assume one transfer in completes the task. But some tasks actually calculate Net Deposit.

Concept:

Net Deposit during campaign = Eligible deposits during campaign − Assets withdrawn during campaign

Example:

You deposit 1,000 USDT during the campaign
Later you withdraw 400 USDT
If the task uses net deposit logic, your net deposit may only be ~600 USDT

So if the task requires maintaining 1,000 USDT net deposit, withdrawing funds before task completion may cause you to fall short.

Whether net deposit is used, the calculation period, and which deposit methods count — all are ultimately governed by that task's terms.
