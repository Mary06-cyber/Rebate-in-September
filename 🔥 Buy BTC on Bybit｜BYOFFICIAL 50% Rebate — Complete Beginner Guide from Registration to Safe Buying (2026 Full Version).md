## 4. Market Buy vs Limit Buy

### 1. Market Buy

Market buy executes at current market price.

Pros:

- Simple
- Fast execution
- Good for quick beginner buys

Cons:

- Possible slippage during volatility
- Actual fill price may differ slightly

For small BTC buys, market orders are intuitive.

### 2. Limit Buy

Limit buy lets you set your desired price.

For example, if you want to buy when BTC pulls back to a certain price, place a limit order.

DCA = Dollar Cost Averaging.

Buy a fixed amount of BTC weekly or monthly.

No need to guess short-term prices. Better for long-term holders.

But DCA is NOT guaranteed profit. It only reduces timing pressure.
