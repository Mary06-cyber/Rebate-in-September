## 8️⃣ Where Does a Bybit Deposit Go?

Crypto deposits are credited to the **Funding Account** by default. Bybit describes it as the main account for managing crypto/fiat assets, deposits, withdrawals, and financial products.

To use funds in another account (e.g., **Unified Trading Account**), Bybit supports auto-transfer or manual internal transfer.

If your USDT deposit shows successful but you can't trade immediately → **check the account location.**

**Logic**: External Wallet/Exchange → Bybit Deposit → Funding Account → Internal Transfer to Trading Account (if needed)

Internal transfers between your own Bybit accounts are NOT blockchain transactions.
