## 🪪 Step 2: KYC identity verification
KYC unlocks buying, deposits, withdrawals and higher limits.
- Fill residence country, nationality, name, date of birth and ID number exactly as on the document
- Upload a valid government-issued original: passport, national ID or driver license, depending on your region's supported list
- ID photo rules: all four corners visible, sharp, no glare, no cropping, no screenshots, not expired; full name and DOB clearly readable
- Selfie/liveness: even lighting, no hat/sunglasses/mask, only one person in frame, follow head-movement prompts; video verification if requested
- Proof of address (some regions/tiers): bank statement or utility bill; many regions require issued within 3 months, EEA may require 6 months—follow the page
- Review: most cases within hours to 24 hours; slower during peaks or re-submission

Common rejections and fixes:
- Blurry/glare/cropped → reshoot on a flat dark surface with front natural light
- Expired or near expiry → use a valid document, no copies

- Face mismatch → clean the lens, remove obstructions, retry liveness
- Camera upload fails → enable camera permission, update the app; back up Web3 seed phrase before reinstalling
