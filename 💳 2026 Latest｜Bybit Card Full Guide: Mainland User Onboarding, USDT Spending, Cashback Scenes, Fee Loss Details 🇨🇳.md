## 2. USDT Spending Deduction Full Chain

When you swipe for 100 EUR equivalent, here is the full backend chain:

1. Merchant POS/online gateway sends debit request to banking channel for 100 EUR
2. Mastercard clearing routes request to Bybit Card issuing processor
3. Bybit checks Funding Account balance, converts 100 EUR to equivalent USDT at current platform rate
4. If card base currency ≠ merchant currency (e.g. card based USD, merchant charges EUR), FX fee triggers at regional rate
5. If Funding holds USDT/non-fiat crypto, additional 0.9% crypto conversion fee applies
6. Authorization may apply fx padding — temporarily freezing slightly more than purchase amount, excess auto-refunded after settlement
7. Deduction completes, merchant receives fiat, your Funding Account reduces by corresponding USDT

Calculation example:
Funding holds only USDC (not USD fiat). Swipe 100 EUR. Bybit rate 1 USDC ≈ 0.93167 EUR.
Base deduction = 100 ÷ 0.93167 = 107.33 USDC
Crypto conversion fee 0.9% = 107.33 × 0.009 = 0.97 USDC
Total = 108.3 USDC
If merchant currency differs from card base, add FX fee on top.
