## 4. Order Types

Market: fills fast at current price, slippage in volatility.  
Limit: fills only at your price, good for cost control.  
Conditional: triggers when price condition met, used for planned entry / TP / SL / breakout.

## 5. Long / Short

Long: expect upside, study long near support.  
Short: expect downside, study short near failed resistance; but a sharp pump can enlarge short loss very fast.
