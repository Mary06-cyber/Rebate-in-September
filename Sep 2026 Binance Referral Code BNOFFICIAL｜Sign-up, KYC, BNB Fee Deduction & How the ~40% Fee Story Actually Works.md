### 3. Download the official app
Official download: https://www.binance.com/en/download

- Android: Google Play or official APK

### 4. Complete KYC
KYC = Know Your Customer. Prepare: full legal name, date of birth, country/region, government-issued ID, face verification. If requested: proof of address.

Common failures: name mismatch, glare/blur on document, expired ID, dark environment, hat/mask during face check.

### 5. Security first, then deposit
- 🔐 Google Authenticator: save recovery key offline
- 🔑 Passkey: enable if supported
- 📧 Anti-Phishing Code: set a string only you know
- 🛡️ Withdrawal Address Whitelist

### 6. Keep a little BNB and enable deduction
App: Profile → Settings → BNB Discount → enable **Use BNB to pay fees**. Web: Fee Level / Fees & Transactions Overview → toggle BNB Discount.

> ✅ No BNB = no BNB fee payment. Keep a small balance as Fee Balance. No need to buy large amounts of BNB just for the discount.

### 7. Small USDT deposit
Sender network must match Binance receive network:

| Network | Notes |

| ERC20 / Ethereum | High compatibility, higher cost |
| BEP20 / BSC | Low cost, both sides must support |

First time: send 10–20 USDT, confirm arrival, then send the rest.

### 8. First spot trade
Trade → Spot → BTC/USDT. Beginners: 50–100 USDT market order. Check Trade History Fee column. If fee is deducted in BNB, BNB Fee Deduction is active.

## Common misconceptions

**Misconception 1: 40% is a guaranteed fixed discount**  
No. Referral rate, BNB discount, product type, VIP, region and campaigns all affect final fee.

**Misconception 2: Enabling BNB deduction guarantees a discount**  
Account needs enough BNB and the trade must be eligible.

**Misconception 3: Lower fees = lower trading risk**  
Fees only affect cost, not price volatility.

**Misconception 4: Adding a referral code after registration is always easy**  
Referral relationships are usually bound at account creation. Already-registered accounts may not be able to add or change a code.
