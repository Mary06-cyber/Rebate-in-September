## 📅 5. Must-Check Info Before Trading US Stocks on Bitget

### 1. Pre/After Market & Closed Sessions

Stock Perps are 24/7, but the underlying US stocks are not. Trading Stock Perps during US market closure: prices may "drift," feed sources may switch, Monday opening gaps, post-earnings gaps.

### 2. Earnings Season

On NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, META earnings nights, Stock Perps may: spike-wick instantly, funding rates go abnormal, liquidation prices get hit rapidly. Beginners: avoid high leverage around earnings.

### 3. Macro Data

- CPI, PCE
- Non-Farm Payrolls
- FOMC
- Initial Jobless Claims
- Treasury Yields
- US Dollar Index (DXY)

Tech stock perpetuals are highly sensitive to rate expectations.

### 4. Quadruple Witching / Quarterly Expiry

While Stock Perps have no expiry, the underlying US stock/ETF/options market has "Quadruple Witching" — liquidity and volatility amplify.
