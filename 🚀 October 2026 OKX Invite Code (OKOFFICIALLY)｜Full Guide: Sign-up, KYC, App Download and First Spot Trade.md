After funding, start with Spot. For example, if you hold USDT and want BTC, open Trade → Spot → BTC/USDT.

In the app: Assets → Transfer

Set To: Trading Account
Select USDT or another asset and confirm the transfer.

Then open the BTC/USDT trading page.

Two common order types for beginners:

Market Order
Buys BTC at the current best available market price. Simple and fast. During high volatility, the executed price may differ from the last quoted price due to slippage.

Limit Order
Buys only when price reaches your specified level. Example: BTC market price is 80,000 USDT but you only want to buy at 78,000 USDT, so place a limit order at 78,000 USDT. It fills only when the market meets your condition.

Open but unfilled orders appear in Open Orders. Filled or cancelled orders appear in Order History.

💡 Note: beginners can start with a small Market Order to learn the interface, then try Limit Order. Always double-check the trading pair and amount before confirming.
