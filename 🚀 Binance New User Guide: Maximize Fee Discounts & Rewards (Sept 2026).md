## 🪙 What is BNB fee discount?

BNB is Binance's native token. Enabling "Use BNB to Pay Fees" lets eligible trades be paid with BNB, unlocking BNB discounts.

Difference from BNOFFICIAL:

## ⚙️ Where to enable BNB deduction?

After login: Profile / Account → Fee Settings / Trading Fees → Use BNB to Pay Fees → Enable

App paths may be labeled: BNB Deduction / Pay fees with BNB. Keywords: BNB + Fee + Deduction.

---

## 💡 Why hold BNB after enabling the toggle?

System deducts BNB from YOUR account balance. 0 BNB → nothing to deduct → original fee rate applies.

No need to hold large BNB amounts — just keep enough for near-term trading fees as a "Fee Balance".

---

## 🧮 How BNB deduction actually works

If a qualifying trade originally costs 5 USDT in fees, enabling BNB payment converts it to equivalent BNB at current price, deducted from Spot wallet. Check Trade History / Order History after trade — Fee column showing BNB means active.

---

## ✅ How to confirm BNB deduction is really on?

Make a small spot trade (e.g., BTC/USDT, 10–20 USDT). After execution, check Fee. If it shows BNB deducted → success. If USDT → re-check toggle / balance / pair eligibility.
