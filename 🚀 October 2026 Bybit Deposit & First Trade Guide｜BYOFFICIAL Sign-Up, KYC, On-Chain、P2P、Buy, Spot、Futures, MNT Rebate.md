## 6️⃣ Funding, UTA and MNT Discount

Deposits land in Funding by default. Spot can run from Funding; futures work best in Unified Trading Account (UTA).

Enable MNT:
1. Move MNT into UTA (spot discount may use the relevant spot account, but derivatives usually use UTA)
2. Spot page → fee preferences → enable "Use MNT for fee discount"
3. Futures page → enable again; Spot and Futures settings are separate
4. Enable each subaccount separately
5. MNT covers opening/closing trade fees only; funding rates, borrow interest and withdrawal fees are not covered
6. MNT payment is not supported on inverse contracts, copy trading, bots, RFQ, API orders or liquidations
