## 14. Futures Trading (High Risk — Don't Rush In)

Path: Trade → Futures → USDT Perpetual

Key terms:
- Long: profit if price rises
- Short: profit if price falls
- Isolated: independent margin per position, loss capped (recommended for beginners)
- Cross: shared margin across positions, riskier in extremes
- Leverage: 3x / 5x / 10x / 20x…
- Liquidation Price: position force-closed when margin insufficient
- Mark Price: used for liquidation calc, NOT latest trade price
- Funding Fee: periodic settlement between Long and Short
- Unrealized PnL: not real profit until position closed

First futures attempt:
USDT perpetual → Isolated → Low leverage (2x~5x) → Small capital → 10~20 USDT test position → MUST set Stop Loss. No SL = gambling.
