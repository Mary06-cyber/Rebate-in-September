- Select fiat and USDT/other coin
- Filter by completion rate, trade count, verified merchant, price, payment method
- Pay only through the in-app order to the merchant’s listed details

- Checkout page shows rate, fee and received amount
- Usually more expensive than P2P/on-chain; good for small instant buys

Bank/fiat path: ACH, SEPA, SWIFT or local transfer where available; supported countries, currencies and settlement times follow the Buy Crypto/fiat deposit page.

💡 Test each channel with a small amount: P2P for spread/merchant quality, card for convenience fee, bank transfer for large/low-cost funding.
