## 4. Spot Account — The Foundation

Spot account holds assets after you buy BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL. Understanding Spot is the prerequisite for everything else.

### What Spot Account Shows

| Info | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Asset name | BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB etc. |
| Holdings | How much you have |
| Valuation | Current USD/fiat equivalent |
| Available | Can trade/withdraw immediately |
| Frozen | In open orders / pending withdrawal / subscribed |
| Trade history | Time, price, qty, fee per trade |
| Deposit history | Every external incoming tx |
| Withdrawal history | Every outgoing tx |

### Spot Trade Flow (BTC/USDT Example)

```
1. You have 100 USDT → Spot account
2. Select BTC/USDT pair
3. Choose "Buy"
4. Order type: Market (instant) or Limit (set price)
5. Amount: e.g. 100 USDT
6. Confirm fee: Spot usually 0.1% (BNB discount available)
7. Submit order
8. After fill: USDT ↓, BTC ↑ in Spot
```

**Core logic: Spot = swap one asset for another, assets stay in your account.**

Binance Earn includes Flexible, Fixed, Locked, DeFi Staking, Launchpool.

### Product Types

| Type | Feature | For |
|------|---------|-----|
| Flexible | Anytime access, floating rate | Beginners wanting liquidity |
| Fixed | Set term, higher rate | Don't need funds soon |
| Locked | Cannot withdraw during term | Long-term holders |
| DeFi Staking | On-chain yield, smart contract risk | Advanced |
| Launchpool | Stake for new token airdrops | New project watchers |

### 7 Things to Check Before Subscribing

1. ✅ Product type: flexible or fixed?
2. ✅ Principal guaranteed? (Earn does NOT guarantee)
3. ✅ Lock period: days? months? early exit?
4. ✅ Early redemption: supported? penalty?
5. ✅ Yield calc: APR or fixed? daily or maturity?
6. ✅ Asset price risk: coin drop may exceed yield
7. ✅ Region restriction: available in your area?

> ⚠️ **Rule: Don't subscribe just because APY looks high. Read rules first.**

## 9. Futures — Learn Last

Futures involve leverage, margin, liquidation price. Not for complete beginners.

### Key Concepts

| Concept | Simple |
|---------|--------|
| Leverage | 100 USDT controls 1000 USDT position (10x) |
| Margin | Min fund to maintain position |
| Long | Bet price up |
| Short | Bet price down |
| Funding | Periodic payment between longs/shorts |
| Isolated | Per-position margin, that pos liquidates |
| Cross | Shared margin, one blow = all blow |
| Liq price | Forced close at this loss level |

**Path:** Register → KYC → Security → USDT → Spot → Asset page → Small test → Futures rules → Demo → Live (very low leverage)
