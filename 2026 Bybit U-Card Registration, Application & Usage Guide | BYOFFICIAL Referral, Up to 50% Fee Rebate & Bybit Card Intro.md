## 1. What Is Bybit U-Card

"U-Card" is community slang; the product is **Bybit Card**. It is not a standalone bank account. When you pay, the card backend takes assets from your Bybit Funding Account or Flexible Savings, sells them at your priority order into the merchant's settlement fiat, and settles via Mastercard or Visa.

- You hold: USDT / USDC / BTC / ETH / XRP / GRAM
- Merchant receives: local fiat (USD/EUR/ARS/BRL/MXN/AUD)
- Middle step: sell-crypto + FX conversion at point of sale

> 💡 It is an off-ramp payment tool, not a yield account, not a universal credit card (some regions issue Mastercard credit variant, but most are prepaid/debit).
