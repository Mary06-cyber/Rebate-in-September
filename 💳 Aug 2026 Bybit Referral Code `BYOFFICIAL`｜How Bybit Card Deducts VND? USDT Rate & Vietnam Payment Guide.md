## 6️⃣ Why is money "frozen" before being officially deducted?

When a Bybit Card transaction is authorized by the merchant, the corresponding amount in the Funding Account may be **held (frozen)** first, then officially deducted when the merchant completes the transaction. According to Bybit, the completion process may take up to **30 days** in some cases.

This is especially common with:
- 🏨 Hotels
- 🚗 Car rentals
- 🧾 Services using pre-authorization
- ⏳ Merchants not settling immediately

If the merchant cancels the authorization, the held fiat will be returned as available balance. Therefore, when viewing Bybit Card history, distinguish between **authorization/hold** and **settled transaction**.
