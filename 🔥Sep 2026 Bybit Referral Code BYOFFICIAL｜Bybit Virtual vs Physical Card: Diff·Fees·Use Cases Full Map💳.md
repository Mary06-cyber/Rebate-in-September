A payment card with no plastic medium. After approval, card number, expiry, CVV show in Bybit Card page.

- Online shopping (marketplaces, brand sites)
- Software & digital services (SaaS, cloud, plugins)
- Subscriptions: ChatGPT, Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, iCloud, Notion

Official note: virtual card auto-activates after successful application, no waiting for delivery.

## 🏪4. What Is Bybit Physical Card?

Real card mailed to KYC address. Beyond online pay, key add-ons are offline:

- Supermarket, convenience, restaurant POS swipe
- Chip insert
- Contactless tap (per scheme/terminal)
- Travel hotel, dining, mall spend
- Supported ATM cash-out (subject to region/limits/operator fee)

Activate path:
Finance → Bybit Card → Manage My Card → Activate
Some regions require physical PIN set.

## 🌍9. When FX Fee Applies?

When transaction currency ≠ Bybit Card denomination currency, usually FX conversion.

APAC sample: card USD, spend at EUR/JPY/GBP merchant → FX fee **2%** (on top of scheme rate).

Overseas real cost = price + scheme rate spread + Bybit FX fee + merchant fee.
