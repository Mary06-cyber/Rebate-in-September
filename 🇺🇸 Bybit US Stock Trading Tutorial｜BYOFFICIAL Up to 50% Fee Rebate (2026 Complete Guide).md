## 📊 3. Common Ways to Trade US Stocks on Bybit

### 1. 📄 Bybit CFD (Contract for Difference)

Core logic of CFD: You don't hold the stock; you trade the price difference.

- Bullish → Go long
- Bearish → Go short
- Features: Flexible, but involves leverage, spreads, fees, and overnight interest
- Beginners should NOT start with large positions

### 2. 🔄 Stock Perpetual Contracts

Similar to crypto perpetual contracts:

- USDT as base currency
- No fixed expiry date
- Long / Short supported
- No direct stock ownership
- Involves funding rates, margin, and liquidation risk

⚠️ This is NOT buying stocks in the traditional sense. It's derivative trading with higher risk than spot purchases.
