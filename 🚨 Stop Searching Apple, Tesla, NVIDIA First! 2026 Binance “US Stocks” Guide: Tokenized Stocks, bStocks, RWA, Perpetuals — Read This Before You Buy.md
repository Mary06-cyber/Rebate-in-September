## 22. Do bStocks Pay Dividends?

Usually yes, but not as cash.

bStocks typically handle dividends through a Multiplier / rebasing mechanism:

- Company pays dividend
- Applicable US withholding tax is deducted
- Net amount is reinvested
- Your displayed bStock balance increases
- Raw on-chain token count may stay the same

Non-US holders may still face ~30% US withholding tax before reinvestment, depending on treaty relief. 

So: economic exposure yes, cash dividend no, voting rights no.
